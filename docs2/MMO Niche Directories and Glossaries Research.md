# MMO Niche Directories and Glossaries Research

## Initial Findings

### Affiliate & Program Directories
1. **Affiliate.Watch** (https://affiliate.watch/) - 865+ programs, categorized by niche, network, and payment method. Features AI ratings and SimilarWeb ranks.
2. **Post Affiliate Pro Directory** (https://www.postaffiliatepro.com/affiliate-program-directory/) - Large directory of affiliate programs.
3. **FlexOffers Directory** (https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-program-directory-list/) - Comprehensive list of programs within their network.
4. **ClickBank Marketplace** (https://www.clickbank.com/marketplace/) - The gold standard for digital product affiliate programs.
5. **ShareASale** (https://www.shareasale.com/) - Major network with a searchable merchant directory.
6. **CJ Affiliate (formerly Commission Junction)** (https://www.cj.com/) - High-end affiliate network directory.
7. **Impact.com** (https://impact.com/) - Modern partnership platform with a massive brand directory.
8. **PartnerStack** (https://partnerstack.com/) - Focuses on SaaS affiliate programs.

### Side Hustle & MMO Resource Sites
9. **Side Hustle Nation** (https://www.sidehustlenation.com/) - Massive directory of side hustle ideas and case studies.
10. **Income Diary** (https://www.incomediary.com/) - High-traffic blog with lists of earning websites.
11. **SideHustleHero** (https://www.sidehustlehero.com/) - Focuses on trending side hustles.
12. **Niche Pursuits** (https://www.nichepursuits.com/) - Case studies and directories of niche site ideas.

### Glossaries
13. **Rewardful Affiliate Glossary** (https://www.rewardful.com/articles/affiliate-marketing-glossary) - 50+ terms.
14. **Post Affiliate Pro Glossary** (https://www.postaffiliatepro.com/affiliate-marketing-glossary/) - Comprehensive industry terms.
15. **ClickBank Glossary** (https://www.clickbank.com/affiliate-marketing-glossary/) - 67+ terms.

### Niche & Category Aggregators
16. **Affiliate Program Database (APDB)** (https://www.affiliateprogramdb.com/) - 500+ categories including:
    - Software as a Service (SaaS)
    - Digital Products
    - Enterprise Software
    - Application Software
    - Recurring Commission Programs
    - Pay Per Lead (CPL)
    - Global Affiliate Programs
17. **StatsDrone** (https://statsdrone.com/affiliate-programs/) - Database filtered by niches like iGaming, Finance, etc.
18. **iDevAffiliate Directory** (https://directory.idevaffiliate.com/) - Searchable directory of programs using iDevAffiliate software.
19. **Success With Soul** (https://successwithsoul.co/list-of-affiliate-programs-bloggers/) - 100+ programs for bloggers.
20. **Beehiiv Blog** (https://www.beehiiv.com/blog/best-niche-for-affiliate-marketing) - List of 100 best niches.
