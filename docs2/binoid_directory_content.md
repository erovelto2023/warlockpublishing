## 2. Key Cannabinoids Defined

### Delta 9 THC
**Definition:** Delta 9 THC (Delta-9-tetrahydrocannabinol) is the primary psychoactive compound found in cannabis. Hemp-derived Delta 9 is legal federally under the 2018 Farm Bill as long as it contains less than 0.3% THC by dry weight.
- **Related Products:** Binoid Delta 9 Gummies, Delta 9 Syrups.
- **How to Start:** Start with a low dose (5-10mg) to assess tolerance.

### THCA (Tetrahydrocannabinolic Acid)
**Definition:** THCA is the non-psychoactive precursor to THC. When heated (decarboxylated), it converts into Delta 9 THC. THCA "High-THC" flower and carts are popular for providing a potent experience when vaped or smoked.
- **Related Products:** Binoid THCA Carts, THCA Diamonds, THCA Flower.
- **How to Start:** Use a compatible 510-thread battery for carts; start with 1-2 small puffs.

### THCP (Tetrahydrocannabiphorol)
**Definition:** THCP is a naturally occurring cannabinoid that is significantly more potent than Delta 9 THC—some studies suggest up to 33 times more active at CB1 receptors. It provides a deep, long-lasting "high."
- **Related Products:** Binoid THCP Vapes, THCP Gummies.
- **How to Start:** Exercise extreme caution; start with a very small amount due to its high potency.
